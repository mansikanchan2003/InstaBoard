## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, enterprise-grade campaign intelligence environment designed for growth operators, media strategists, and performance analysts. Built on the intersection of analytical rigour and vibrant brand energy, the aesthetic merges precision modernism with functional density.

The visual narrative balances authoritative deep slates with an electric, warm amber-gold core. It communicates total operational reliability, real-time lucidity, and intelligent automation. Visual noise is stripped down in favor of crisp bounding containers, high-contrast structural dividers, tabular data legibility, and contextual micro-badges. The emotional resonance is focused, premium, and proactive: complex multi-vector Instagram attribution feels instantaneous, frictionless, and definitive.

## Colors

The color system reflects strict semantic hierarchy designed for fast-paced analytical comprehension.

- **Primary Amber (`#F5A623`):** Reserved for primary interactive anchors, KPI trend callouts, and decisive focal points. Hover state shifts deeper to `#D97706` for clear tactile feedback.
- **Secondary Deep Slate (`#0F172A`):** The structural backbone, used for top navigation chrome, primary headers, dark utility toolbars, and high-impact textual data.
- **Tertiary Sky (`#0284C7`):** Powers secondary data visualizations, comparative trend lines, and state-level informational indicators.
- **Neutral Core (`#64748B`):** Provides accessible secondary typography, structural borders (`#E2E8F0`), and baseline container framing.
- **Status & Intelligence Semantics:** 
  - *Active:* Forest Emerald (`#10B981`) against soft mint (`#ECFDF5`).
  - *Paused:* Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) against light amber tint (`#FFFBEB`).
  - *Draft:* Muted Slate (`#64748B`) against flat grey (`#F1F5F9`).
  - *Completed:* Informational Sky (`#0284C7`) against cool ice (`#F0F9FF`).
  - *AI Inference:* Distinct Indigo (`#6366F1`) paired with violet tint (`#EEF2FF`) and border tone (`#C7D2FE`) to separate algorithmic extrapolations from raw telemetry.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intentional split: **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers structural personality and authority across section titles, viewports, and primary modular headers, while **Inter** delivers high-density, fatigue-free analytical readability across table structures, telemetry, and interactive elements.

- **Tabular Alignment:** All metric values, timestamps, and volume aggregations must apply OpenType `tnum` (tabular figures) and `cv01` alternates to avoid visual baseline jitter during dynamic data polling.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize tightened tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to project modern crispness. Micro labels, chips, and AI indicators use uppercase tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) for immediate recognition at reduced optical sizes.
- **Weight Pairing:** Bold weights (`700`, `800`) are strictly reserved for top-level performance indicators and page targets to preserve rapid visual scanning across dense dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a compact, data-first fluid grid prioritizing vertical hierarchy and minimal whitespace overhead.

- **Canvas Architecture:** An asymmetrical fluid grid featuring a fixed collapsible control column (240px wide on desktop), paired with a multi-column analytical dashboard surface (12-column dynamic grid, 1.5rem gutters).
- **Density Philosophy:** Padding scales are condensed. Metrics tiles, filter rows, and data tables utilize tight internal bounds (`space-sm` and `space-md`), minimizing scroll distance and contextual fragmentation.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - *Mobile (< 768px):* Single-column vertical stack, 1rem margins, side drawer for system navigation, simplified card wrappers for tabular data.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1024px):* 6-column fluid structure, 1rem gutters, metrics grouped into 2x2 grids.
  - *Desktop (> 1024px):* Full 12-column layout, multi-card comparison rails, 2rem edge canvas margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system favors **structural surface contrast and razor-sharp border outlines** over heavy drop shadows. Depth informs functional layering rather than decorative illusion.

- **Surface Level 0 (App Canvas):** Soft, cool slate `#F8FAFC`. All metrics and control interfaces sit on this foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a deliberate 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadows are absent or exceptionally subtle: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Flyouts, Menus & Toolbars):** `#FFFFFF` anchored with `border: 1px solid #CBD5E1` and an ambient boundary shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Global Overlays & Modals):** Pure white backed by an enterprise backdrop barrier of `#0F172A` at 40% opacity, paired with an elevated directional drop shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive Focus States:** Active or selected elements replace neutral gray borders with high-contrast `#0F172A` or `#F5A623`, maintaining crisp 1px sharpness without outer glowing fuzziness.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Crisp 0.25rem (4px) to 0.375rem (6px) radii govern components, maintaining a sharp, precise, enterprise dashboard feel.

- **Containers & KPI Cards:** Use 6px (`rounded-md`) corner radii to maintain tight spatial containment without appearing sterile or brutalist.
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Select Menus:** Calibrated at 4px (`0.25rem`) to 6px (`0.375rem`) for compact alignment with adjacent border strokes.
- **Status Pills & AI Badges:** Apply fully rounded ends (`9999px`) to create an immediate shape-driven distinction between structural data cards and micro-status chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid warm amber (`#F5A623`) with high-contrast text (`#0F172A` bold), 4px border radius, padding `0.5rem 1rem`. Focus state shows a 2px offset ring in deep slate.
- **Secondary / Neutral:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E2E8F0` border, text `#0F172A`. On hover: surface shifts to `#F8FAFC`, border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive:** Muted text with transparent background; destructive variants feature `#EF4444` hover tint.

### Badges & Status Pills
- **Geometry:** Height 22px, `rounded-full` (pill-shaped), font size 11px, weight 600, padding `2px 8px`.
- **Active:** Background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`. Accompanied by a 6px solid emerald dot.
- **Paused:** Background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
- **Draft:** Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#475569`.
- **Completed:** Background `#F0F9FF`, border `#BAE6FD`, text `#0369A1`.
- **AI Badges:** Background `#EEF2FF`, border `#C7D2FE`, text `#4338CA`. Prefixed with an automated 12px spark icon, tracked uppercase at `+0.06em` (`AI-ESTIMATED`, `AI-INFERRED`).

### Cards & KPI Tiles
- Bounded by 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, corner radius 6px, padding `1rem 1.25rem`.
- Layout places the descriptive metric label and trend delta at the top, bold tabular stat in the center (`metric-stat-lg`), and secondary sparkline or historical baseline comparison anchored at the bottom edge.

### Data Tables
- Row height: 44px (dense) or 52px (standard).
- Table header: Background `#F8FAFC`, text `#475569`, uppercase, font size 11px, weight 600, letter spacing `0.05em`, border bottom 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- Cells: Text `#0F172A`, font size 13px, numeric values aligned right with `font-feature-settings: "tnum"`.
- Row hover: Background shifts seamlessly to `#F8FAFC`.

### Form Controls & Filters
- **Inputs:** Height 36px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text size 13px, padding `0 0.75rem`. Focus ring: 1px `#0F172A`.
- **Segmented Controls:** Enclosed container in `#F1F5F9` with active pill in `#FFFFFF` containing an inset outline and subtle elevation.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16x16px bounding frame, `#CBD5E1` border; active states fill with `#0F172A` displaying a crisp white mark.
